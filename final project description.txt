The final project will be an extension of the Assignment 7 Alligator Fishing Game (Group 9). The game in its A7 form 
featured a user-controlled alligator chasing in an aquarium simulation. The alligator is controlled using A (left), 
W (up), D (right), S (down), and space (mouth open/close). The goal of the game is for the alligator to catch a fish 
within the time limit of 30 seconds. If the timer finishes without the fish being caught, the player loses. Extra time 
leftover at the end of the level is equivalent to the number of points a player earns.

To this, we added:
- Keyboard interactivity with muting/sound control keystroke (Akhila). Press M to mute, . to increase sound and , to 
decrease sound. Sound attributions are listed below. 
- Smoothed the alligator movement and tail swishing (Akhila)
